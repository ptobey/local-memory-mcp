# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This project aims to provide a respectful, technically rigorous, and inclusive environment for everyone participating in issues, pull requests, and discussions.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Focus on technical facts and reproducible details.
- Assume good intent, ask clarifying questions, and critique ideas, not people.
- Keep feedback actionable.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or conduct
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith engagement
- Sharing private/confidential information without consent

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests
- Discussions and review comments
- Other project-managed communication channels

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject content that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

## Reporting

To report conduct issues, contact the repository maintainer privately via the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Please include links/screenshots and enough context to investigate.
